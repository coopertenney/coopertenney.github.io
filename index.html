  • 2026 — now

    Stanford Football — Player Valuation Tool

    Built to the Assistant GM's PRD · April 2026 – present

    Takes a recruit profile and returns calibrated year-by-year probabilities of four career outcomes (bust / depth / starter / impact), then a dollar valuation of a proposed NIL offer. Trained on 45k+ recruit profiles and 230k player-seasons from CollegeFootballData plus PFF grades. Replaced circular percentile labels with absolute thresholds, which exposed a real risk spread across eligibility years, and rebuilt retention curves for the transfer-portal era. Ships as a single self-contained HTML file for projecting in a room.

    • TypeScript
    • ordinal regression
    • CFBD + PFF
    • AUC 0.72 vs 0.69 baseline

  • 2026

    Quantifying the Caitlin Clark Externality

    DATASCI 112 final project

    A difference-in-differences study isolating one player's effect on WNBA attendance and revenue from league-wide growth. Scraped four seasons of WNBA and NBA data; tuned KNN, random forest, and XGBoost via 5-fold CV to a 1,590-ticket RMSE on pre-breakout data. Found $32M in surplus ticket revenue and 8,600+ additional fans per game — value the Indiana Fever captured and the player did not.
